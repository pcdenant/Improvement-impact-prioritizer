Map the current state, place your kaizen bursts on it, and see which one cuts the lead time and the P85 the most. Other version: distribution-based prioritizer.

Your value stream today

Steps. All times are in calendar days, like your lead time. Process time = calendar days of real work on one item. Inventory = items waiting in or before the step. Wait = inventory ÷ throughput (Little's law); enter the wait directly only if you have no inventory count.

2 · KAIZEN BURSTS

What does each action change on the map?

Write the future-state number for each box an action changes, pessimistic / probable / optimistic. Example: inventory in Analysis 40 → 18 / 14 / 10. “Throughput” changes every wait at once. Set process time and inventory to 0 to remove a step.

3 · FUTURE STATE

Ranked by P85 gain (probable)

Bar = share of the gap to the target P85 that the burst closes. Full bar = target reached. Thin line = pessimistic → optimistic range. Tick bursts to build the future-state map.

Your data stays in this browser (saved automatically). Nothing is sent anywhere.

How it works: classic future-state VSM. Lead time = Σ process time + Σ wait, with wait = inventory ÷ throughput (Little's law). Flow efficiency = process time ÷ lead time. The P85 and median are projected by scaling the real values with the lead-time ratio (future ÷ current): this assumes the tail shrinks in proportion.
